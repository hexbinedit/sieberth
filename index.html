// Paul Sieberth

Built to Tinker.

// Breathe in — learn. Breathe out — build.

I've spent years working in cybersecurity — building detection pipelines, hunting adversaries through cloud infrastructure, and turning raw log data into something actionable. Finding solutions is less a skill than a habit; I tend to think several steps ahead, looking for ways to automate the repetitive, eliminate the unnecessary, and build things that don't need to be rebuilt.

Outside of security I'm a builder in the broadest sense. I have a long history with film photography and pick up my digital camera to document things worth remembering — Mardi Gras, live music, the kind of moments that reward a fast lens and good timing. I cook from scratch, learn wherever I can, and tend to go deep on whatever subject earns my attention. I believe the best work comes from people who can think across disciplines, and I've spent a long time deliberately becoming that kind of person. Lately that includes working seriously with AI and large language models — not as a novelty but as a force multiplier for building things that are faster and capable of providing new insights.

I'm not afraid to tackle hard problems. Give me a clear goal, room to think, and access to the right tools. Or, if needed, I'll build those too.
